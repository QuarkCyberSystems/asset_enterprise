## Still open

The independent GL report detects differences but does not convert the operational value model. Historical acquisition attribution and transaction lifecycle coverage must be resolved and tested before replacing that model. No fallback to the fold is disguised as a GL-derived value.
2. **R135:** Control Category depreciation versus project settlement needs the business rule. This patch changes asset_enterprise only; it does not exclude rows in project_accounting or prohibit control depreciation.
3. **R126:** The meaning of a blank project on an asset transfer is still undecided. No project-exit behavior was invented.
4. **R201/R202:** Existing living-target life extension and CM Material Issue/Receipt behavior remain implemented. Commit bodies corroborate the recorded 19/08 and 25/08 client directions, but do not themselves sign v2.18. They still need explicit registration and confirmation; proposed wording is below.
5. **CH-12 finance confirmation / R021/R025/R026/R185:** Literal depreciation cases remain red. Expectations were not rewritten to make the result green.
6. **R174:** Restore now uses native reversal identity. The separate reverse_merge / core AVA / Repair back-reference findings are not all resolved by that change.
7. Remaining design/document omissions (R095/R027/R150, TC-029/049, VR-024, §12.13), built-untested cases, and sign-off dependencies remain open. The original trace statuses and signed design were not silently rewritten.
8. E-22/E-24 project-dimension coverage needs a suitable project fixture; full UAT and production migration remain outstanding.
